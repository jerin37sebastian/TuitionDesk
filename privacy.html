Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you use TuitionDesk, we collect:

  • Your name, email address and password (for your account)
  • Tuition center details (name, city, phone)
  • Student information you enter (name, class, batch, parent contact)
  • Attendance and fee records you create

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Provide and operate the TuitionDesk service
  • Allow you to manage your students, attendance and fees
  • Send important service updates if needed

3. Data Storage

All your data is securely stored using Google Firebase (Firestore), hosted on Google Cloud servers. Your data is only accessible to you — no other TuitionDesk user can see your center's data.

4. We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information or your students' data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

We use Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules to ensure only you can access your center's data. All data is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted).

6. Your Rights

You can:

  • Access and update your information anytime from Settings
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Export your data on request

7. Children's Data

TuitionDesk is a tool for tuition center administrators (adults). Student records are entered by the center admin. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children.
